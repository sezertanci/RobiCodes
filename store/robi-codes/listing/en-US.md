## Tam açıklama (en fazla 4000 karakter)

Robi Codes teaches children aged 7–12 the core ideas of coding through play. There is no typing code on a screen; children line up commands, watch how the robot moves and find their own mistakes.

FOUR GAMES, FOUR KEY IDEAS
• Find the Robot's Path: Guide the robot to its goal with direction commands.
• Order the Steps: Put the steps in the right order and learn how algorithms work.
• If This, Then: Choose the right command for each situation and discover conditions.
• Repeat It: Do repeated moves with a single block and get to know loops.

80 LEVELS THAT GROW STEP BY STEP
Each game has four difficulty groups – easy, medium, hard and expert – for 80 levels in total. Children who get stuck can ask for a hint, and replay a level to collect more stars.

REWARDS AND CHARACTERS
• Earn stars, badges and trophies as you finish levels.
• Pick your favourite robot character from Robi and friends.

FOR FAMILIES
• No ads.
• No account needed; no personal data is collected or shared.
• Progress is stored only on the device, and the games work without an internet connection.
• In-app purchases only start after a question that only an adult can answer.
• Play in English or Turkish.
